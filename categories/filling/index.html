SELECTION CONSIDERATIONS

Describe the material at the point of filling.

Define a Filling or Dosing Application ↗

Specify viscosity or consistency at the working temperature, along with any particles and the required quantity. These are selection inputs, not evidence that a listed machine handles every formulation.

Provide bottle opening and height as well as the line layout. Where a listing has multiple head configurations, keep the filling range and container data attached to the selected entry.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • What is the material condition at filling?
  • Which quantity and container opening are required?
  • How are containers supplied and transferred?

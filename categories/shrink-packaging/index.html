SELECTION CONSIDERATIONS

Specify the complete package envelope.

Plan Shrink, Carton Sealing, Strapping and Coding Tasks ↗

Provide the dimensions of the complete item or bundle, including trays and supports. Explain whether a film-sealing stage already exists; a tunnel listing should not be treated as a complete wrapping line.

Identify the intended film and presentation. Discuss loading, discharge and available power with the selected configuration rather than selecting a system from tunnel appearance alone.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • Is the pack a single item or a bundle?
  • Which sealing steps are already available?
  • What are the film, supply and transfer requirements?

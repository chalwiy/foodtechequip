Selecting a Bag or Tube Sealing Process ↗

Ultrasonic joining uses mechanical vibration at the joint. Polymer or fibre composition, coatings and layer structure are important inputs when discussing a bag-sealing task.

Send a material sample and seam drawing, including the joint position and access needed around the working head.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • What are the fibre or polymer and layer structure?
  • What seam shape and access are required?
  • Can you provide an actual bag sample?

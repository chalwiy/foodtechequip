SELECTION CONSIDERATIONS

A material category is not a complete joint specification.

Selecting a Bag or Tube Sealing Process ↗

Ultrasonic joining uses mechanical vibration at the joint. Whether a particular bag can be joined depends on its construction and the proposed process, not merely the description 'plastic' or 'nonwoven'.

Send layered-material details and a drawing of the required seam. Keep the machine's listed applications separate from assumptions about every coating or laminate.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • What are the fibre or polymer and layer structure?
  • What seam shape and access are required?
  • Can you provide an actual bag sample?

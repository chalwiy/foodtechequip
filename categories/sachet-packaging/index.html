Define a Filling or Dosing Application ↗

Powder, granules, liquids and sauces require different feeding arrangements. Share a material sample, target portion and seal layout to define the finished sachet.

Compare packaging rates and film dimensions for the selected configuration. Distinguish roll width, finished bag width and bag length in your drawing.

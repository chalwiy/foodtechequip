SELECTION CONSIDERATIONS

Evaluate the container, cap and liner together.

Matching Containers, Closures and Sealing Equipment ↗

Provide the container, cap and liner as a complete set. Liner construction and container material together define the sealing combination.

For conveyor arrangements, include container height, spacing and the closure shape to define presentation beneath the working head.

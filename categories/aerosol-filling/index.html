How to Prepare an Equipment Enquiry ↗

Provide the paint safety information, can-and-valve format and filling operation required. Include the hazardous-area and pressure requirements from the site assessment.

Aerosol processes may involve flammable substances and pressure hazards. Have qualified personnel assess the formulation, process and site requirements before equipment selection.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • Which formulation and process stage are intended?
  • What can and valve format is used?
  • What site assessment and equipment requirements apply?

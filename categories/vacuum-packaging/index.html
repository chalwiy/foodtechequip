Choosing a Vacuum Packaging Layout ↗

For chamber layouts, provide the entire bagged-product dimensions and loading arrangement. For external layouts, describe support at the sealing position.

Provide bag construction and seal requirements together with actual pack samples. Discuss any product shelf-life requirements as part of the wider product and packaging process.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • What is the filled-pack size and weight?
  • How will it be loaded and supported?
  • What bag construction and closing result are required?

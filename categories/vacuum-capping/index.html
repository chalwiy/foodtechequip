SELECTION CONSIDERATIONS

Use the actual jar and cap pair.

Matching Containers, Closures and Sealing Equipment ↗

Provide the jar finish and closure, including its liner or gasket. Similar outer diameters do not establish that two closures use the same application arrangement.

Define the loading sequence and requested process result. A station count or a vacuum label alone is not a promise of closure integrity or product shelf life.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • Which jar finish and matching cap are intended?
  • What station and operator arrangement is required?
  • How will the finished closure be evaluated?

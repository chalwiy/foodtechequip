SELECTION CONSIDERATIONS

The filled bag matters as much as the sealing section.

Selecting a Bag or Tube Sealing Process ↗

State the filled weight, bag height and space above the product. These details help define how a bag reaches the sealing position and remains supported during transfer.

Where vacuum or gas flushing is listed, describe the required process separately. A sealing-rate figure does not establish a residual-gas target or a shelf-life result.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • What is the bag construction and mouth width?
  • How is the filled bag held and transferred?
  • Is gas handling actually part of the required process?

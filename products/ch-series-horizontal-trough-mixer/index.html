THE EQUIPMENT

Product overview

The CH series combines a covered horizontal mixing trough, an internal mixing shaft and a stainless steel housing. Choose from CH-10, CH-20, CH-50, CH-100 and CH-200 according to your material, intended batch charge and available space. The smaller models have a different housing and control arrangement from the larger models.

Features

  • Covered mixing trough with lid and handle.
  • Internal mixing shaft and blades.
  • Stainless steel housing.
  • The larger control-panel layout includes start, stop, emergency stop, time control, reset and discharge functions. Panel layout varies by model.

Choose the right batch size

Start with the target charge per batch, bulk density, moisture content and material consistency. Nominal trough volume describes vessel size; the working charge depends on the material and operating conditions.

Share the mixing result you need and how it will be assessed, so batch size and mixing time can be discussed together.

Plan loading and transfer

Describe how material will enter the trough, how each batch will be emptied and how it reaches the next dosing or filling step. Include the transfer method, receiving equipment and available space in your enquiry.

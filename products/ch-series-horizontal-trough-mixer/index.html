SELECTION DETAILS

Configure the mixer for your material

Start with the mass you need to mix per batch. Trough volume describes the vessel size. Working batch weight also depends on bulk density, moisture and how the material moves when mixed.

Describe the result you need and how you will measure it. Include your available power supply, installation space and any access requirements for loading or cleaning.

Plan loading and discharge

Describe how you will feed the trough and where the mixed batch needs to go. Include the receiving bin or equipment, its loading height and how you plan to transfer the material.

Where this task fits in your process

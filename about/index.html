About FTE

Food Tech Equipment Co., Ltd. operates under the FTE brand, supplying and exporting food production equipment from Xuzhou, Jiangsu, China.

Equipment supply with a practical focus.

FTE supplies and exports food production equipment from Xuzhou, Jiangsu, China. We help customers explore equipment options and discuss the configurations relevant to their production requirements.

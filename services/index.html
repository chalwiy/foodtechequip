PRACTICAL SUPPORT

Equipment is one part.
Support is another.

From selection to equipment use, discuss the support you need with FTE.

01

Equipment selection

Start with a material sample, finished-pack dimensions and the output required at that format. Identify the configuration being considered and the operations before and after it.

02

Installation guidance

Prepare the available power supply, working space, access and transfer-height information. Discuss installation guidance for the actual equipment and its site interfaces.

03

Training

Describe the tasks your operators will perform and the areas where they need explanation. Tie the training discussion to the controls, configuration and handling sequence of the equipment purchased.

04

Spare parts supply

Identify the equipment, configuration and part concerned. Clear photographs of the component, its position and identification marks help define a spare-parts enquiry.

05

Remote after-sales support

Explain the task being attempted, what happens and any displayed message. Include equipment identification and clear images when discussing the remote assistance needed.

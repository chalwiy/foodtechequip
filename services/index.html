PRACTICAL SUPPORT

Equipment selection
& practical support.

From selection to equipment use, discuss the support you need with FTE.

01

Equipment selection

We help compare equipment configurations for your material, pack format and required output. Include samples, dimensions and the operations before and after the machine.

02

Installation guidance

Installation guidance covers the selected equipment and its site requirements. Share the available electrical supply, working space, access and transfer heights.

03

Training

Training focuses on the controls, operator tasks and handling sequence of the equipment purchased. Describe the tasks your operators need to learn.

04

Spare parts supply

We support spare-parts enquiries using the equipment identification, configuration and component details. Clear photographs and identification marks help identify the part required.

05

Remote after-sales support

Remote after-sales support starts with the equipment details and a clear description of the issue. Include the operating task, observed behaviour and any displayed message.

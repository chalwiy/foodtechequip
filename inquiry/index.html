EQUIPMENT ENQUIRIES

Prepare your requirements.

Tell us the equipment you are considering, your material characteristics, packaging dimensions, required output, power supply and destination.

For filling equipment, include target fill volume and container details. For packaging equipment, include the bag format, packaging material and pack weight.

Compare like-for-like configurations

Identify the relevant model, head count or station arrangement. Compare its rate, filling range and dimensions with the target quantity and pack format you need.

Describe the handovers

Explain how material or containers reach the equipment and how the finished pack is collected. Include operator involvement, working height and the available installation space.

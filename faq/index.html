A FEW PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Need information about a particular machine? Contact us with the equipment name and your requirements.

What equipment does FTE supply?

FTE supplies and exports food production equipment, including mixing, filling, dosing, sealing and packaging equipment. Browse by equipment category or start with your application.

How do I compare machines in the same category?

Compare the model or configuration for your material and package. Look at its working arrangement, dimensions, electrical requirements and output at the intended pack size. Include material feed, operator access and collection in the comparison.

Can you help me select equipment?

Yes. Share your material, target batch or portion, packaging format, required output, power supply and destination so we can discuss equipment selection.

What samples or drawings should I prepare?

Provide the material description and finished-pack dimensions. Include matching containers and closures, or the complete construction and required seam for bags and tubes. For mixing, include material characteristics and target batch weight.

How should I specify materials and packaging?

Describe the formulation or material construction, working temperature and any particles, coatings or layers. Actual samples help define the handling and processing requirements for the selected configuration.

How should I compare production rates?

Compare rates for the same portion, pack size and configuration. Include head count, station arrangement, material condition and operator handling when specifying the output you need.

What should I include in a spare-parts enquiry?

Identify the equipment and configuration, then describe the part and its position. Include clear photographs and any identification marks to help identify the component.

What information helps with remote support?

Send the equipment identification, a description of the task, the observed behaviour and any displayed message. Clear photographs help explain the issue and the assistance needed.

What support is available?

Equipment selection, Installation guidance, Training, Spare parts supply, Remote after-sales support.

What are the payment terms?

Payment is by T/T with a 30% deposit.

When will the equipment be dispatched?

Dispatch within one month of contract signing. Transit time to the destination is additional.

How can I request a quotation?

How can the application guides help me?

Use the guides to identify material, packaging and handling details for your enquiry. Follow the relevant equipment-category links to compare configurations, then contact FTE with your requirements.

Planning Shaped Sachets for Liquids and Sauces

A shaped sachet combines a material portion with a specific film outline and sealing area. FTE’s shaped-sachet product provides the equipment starting point; the finished package must be discussed for the intended liquid or sauce.

Begin with what the sachet contains

Describe the liquid or sauce at filling, target portion and any ingredients affecting flow. Distinguish the requested dose from the pack’s outside dimensions. A silhouette can suggest a presentation, but the filled area and surrounding seal still have to suit the actual product.

Describe the contour as a package

Supply a drawing showing the finished cut outline, the intended sealed border and the area available for filling. Mark narrow sections and curves that matter to the design. This is a more useful starting point than a decorative rendering with no dimensions.

Make extra features identifiable

Opening behaviour, print alignment and a pouring point may be important to your project. Mark them as requirements and discuss their feasibility with the proposed configuration. Keep those requests separate from what is already described for the film-fed vertical machine.

Keep equipment and consumables in scope

Include the intended film specification and identify whether a single shape or several formats will be used. Clarify what the equipment quotation should cover and whether consumable supply is a separate request. Evaluate the complete pack and material before finalising the artwork or assuming one setup serves every contour.

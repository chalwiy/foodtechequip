Vacuum Closing of Filled Glass Jars

Vacuum jar closing is a specific packaging task for a matching container-and-closure set. Compare the actual filled condition and operator handling before choosing between the available station arrangements.

Start with a complete jar-and-cap set

Provide the glass jar, matching closure and sealing component together. The neck finish and cap engagement define a different interface from a bag seal or an induction foil liner. Describe the closing result you want so the appropriate category stays clear.

Describe filling before closing

Include the product condition, fill level and temperature at the time the jar reaches the capper. Show any material likely to contact the sealing surface. Headspace and closure requirements should refer to your actual package and closure-supplier instructions, rather than a generic setting.

Compare the loading arrangement

The range includes semi-automatic equipment and two-station and four-station arrangements. Show how jars and caps are presented and how the closed jars are removed. Compare the corresponding electrical and format information for the chosen configuration rather than combining rows.

Keep the preservation process separate

Define the closure checks and what happens to the jar afterwards. The equipment enquiry can address closing and handling; shelf life and any subsequent treatment need a separate, product-specific basis. Avoid using a closed-jar appearance as a substitute for the required package evaluation.

INGREDIENTS TO BATCH

Batch Mixing and Transfer Before Portioning

Plan batch mixing as a complete operation: receiving ingredients, mixing the charge and transferring it to the next step. The CH trough mixer range provides the equipment reference; your material and intended result define the selection discussion.

Define the batch before the vessel

State the target mass and describe the material in its normal condition. Include moisture, consistency and bulk density where known, with units. The nominal trough volume is not the same measure as a working charge. Do not convert the CH model number into a guaranteed batch weight.

Compare the selected CH model

Use the five-model comparison to consider volume, electrical supply, listed charge and dimensions together. The smaller units have a different housing and control arrangement from the larger models. Describe the operation you require rather than assuming all controls shown for one model are present across the series.

Include loading and discharge

Show how ingredients enter the trough and what receives the batch when it is emptied. Identify operator access, receiving height and the route to any separate dosing step. Identify whether transfer is manual or requires additional handling equipment, and include that request in the quotation.

Specify the result and preparation between batches

Describe how mixing success will be assessed and whether materials change between batches. Include the preparation and cleaning requirements of the formulation in the enquiry. The listed cycle is a reference for the range, not a guarantee that every recipe reaches its required result in the same time.

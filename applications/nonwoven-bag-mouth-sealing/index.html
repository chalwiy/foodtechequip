Ultrasonic Closing of Nonwoven and Layered Bag Mouths

A bag-mouth joint combines material layers, seam geometry and access to the sealing head. For ultrasonic closing, identify what meets at the joint before comparing bag dimensions or equipment layouts.

Describe the material at the seam

The term nonwoven describes a construction, not one universal polymer. Include fibre identity, thermoplastic content, coatings and laminates when known. The material that touches at a folded mouth can differ from the visible outside surface.

Match the bag to a relevant equipment record

FTE has ultrasonic-bag products described for nonwoven fabric, fabric bags and mixed bag constructions. These are useful starting points for a sample discussion. Choose the relevant record and provide the complete mouth construction rather than treating every fabric or film as interchangeable.

Show positioning and support

Mark the joint on a bag drawing and describe folds, gussets and any changes in thickness. Explain how the filled bag is supported and how the mouth reaches the working head. The closing area must be considered together with operator access, not as an isolated flat material patch.

Define the intended closure

State whether the seam is intended as the final closure or one part of a larger bag construction. Describe the result and assessment method required for your pack. Include normal joint conditions in sample discussions and assess the result against your intended closure requirement.

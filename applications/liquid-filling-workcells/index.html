WORKCELL & HANDLING

Container Presentation Around a Liquid Filling Workcell

A filling workcell includes the material inlet, the bottle at the nozzle and the work needed to move that bottle onward. Compare compact and conveyor-based equipment using this complete sequence, alongside the intended liquid and quantity.

Start with container presentation

Identify where an empty bottle comes from, how its opening reaches the filling position and what supports it. If an operator places it, describe that action and the access needed. If conveyor presentation is requested, define the bottle spacing and handover rather than assuming every feeding step is included.

Compare the actual equipment arrangement

The compact ceramic-pump unit has an adjustable nozzle and touchscreen. The twin-head pump product has adjustable heads, tubing and a conveyor, while the four-head machine has its own configuration data. These records provide different starting points for layout discussion; they are not interchangeable descriptions of one filling system.

Make the material path explicit

Show where the liquid is held and how it reaches the equipment inlet. Include the product condition and any cleaning or material-change requirements in the brief. A pump label alone does not specify every product-contact component, cleaning method or formulation it can handle.

Follow the bottle after filling

State whether filled bottles are removed individually or transferred to another task. Separate capping, foil sealing or collection equipment from the requested filler. Compare the complete cycle at the same fill and container format so operator movements and the next operation remain visible in the scope.

Shrink Wrapping Individual Items and Supported Bundles

Shrink packaging brings a film arrangement and a heating stage together around a defined package. Start with the finished wrap and complete product envelope, then compare the FTE layout that provides the required steps.

Individual item or grouped bundle?

An individual package may need a surrounding film wrap, while a grouped bundle may use a sleeve and supporting tray. Describe the intended openings and presentation. Group stability and the way the item rests on a conveyor matter alongside the film around it.

Choose the stages to include

The chamber unit, separate film-sealing and shrink line, sleeve line and standalone tunnel represent different scopes. If a film-sealing step already exists, identify it when discussing a tunnel. If the full wrapping task is new, explain loading, film closing, shrinking and collection together.

Compare the complete loaded outline

Show every item in its intended position, with supports, protrusions and travel direction. Use that outline to discuss working clearance and support. A consumable film-width figure answers a different question from whether the complete bundle can pass through the working area.

Keep film and product presentation together

Provide the film specification and the finished appearance required. Discuss the product’s handling and heat-exposure constraints for the actual configuration. Include the loading sequence so the proposed layout addresses the work around the machine as well as the shrinking stage.

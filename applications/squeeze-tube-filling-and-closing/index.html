Filling Squeeze Tubes and Closing Their Open Tails

A squeeze-tube process may need filling and tail sealing together, or only closure of an already-filled tube. Define the starting tube, product and required end finish before choosing equipment.

Identify the tube at entry

Show the empty or filled tube as it arrives, including the capped dispensing end and open tail. Give its construction and dimensions. The end being closed is important: tail sealing is different from cap application at the other end.

Choose the operation scope

The FTE range includes a PE-tube filling-and-sealing product, a two-head combined configuration and plastic-tube ultrasonic sealers. Compare a standalone closing task with a combined filling task explicitly. Identify the work already performed before the tube reaches the requested equipment.

Match package and material

Supply an actual tube sample with its layer specification, along with the product condition and target fill. Describe the tail area available for closure. If the tube is decorated, mark the required orientation and end finish rather than assuming that alignment, trimming or printing functions are included.

Describe loading and collection

Explain how tubes are held, introduced and removed at each step. Include required output at the intended fill and format. Discuss the complete operation for the particular packaging construction, product and intended use.

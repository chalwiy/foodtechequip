MATERIAL TO PORTION

Powder and Granule Portioning into Bags or Containers

Specify the portion, receiving pack and handling method before comparing dry-product dosing equipment. Metering material and closing its package may be separate operations.

Describe the normal material condition

For powder, explain how it arrives at the hopper and what you observe during transfer. For granules, include size variation and the condition of the actual production material. The catalogue has auger powder dosing and hopper-fed granule weighing configurations; select the relevant route without assuming all dry products feed in the same way.

Keep the portion and checking method explicit

State whether the target is mass or volume, with units and acceptable variation. Include the smallest and largest portions needed. Identify how each filled portion will be checked. Discuss the checking requirement alongside the controls of the selected configuration.

Design the receiving task

Send the bag or container opening, height and support arrangement. Describe who positions it and what happens when a dose finishes. If several pack sizes are used, include the most restrictive opening as well as the largest envelope; a target portion alone does not define access below an outlet.

Plan the move to closure

Explain whether filled packs are removed individually or transferred to another operation. Where bag sealing is requested, provide the bag construction and filled dimensions. The weighing-and-bag-sealing product is a related option to discuss, with its actual transfer and operator tasks kept clear in the quotation.

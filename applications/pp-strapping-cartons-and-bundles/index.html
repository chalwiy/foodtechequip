PP Strapping of Cartons and Supported Bundles

A strapping enquiry should describe the load, the strap route and the work needed to apply each band. FTE’s arch and side-seal products provide different arrangements to discuss for that task.

Separate restraint from carton closure

A strap follows a path around the package. Tape closes particular carton seams. Describe the function you need instead of assuming one operation replaces the other. Where both are requested, show the order and the condition of the package arriving at strapping.

Show the loaded package and its support

Provide the complete carton or bundle outline with the travel direction and support underneath. Mark every strap position and the preferred joint location. The side-seal machine also has an arch frame, so consider clearance and joining position together rather than choosing by the name alone.

Include the PP consumable

Identify the strap width, thickness and supplier specification for your intended PP strap. Discuss the load and surface condition with the selected equipment. A broad material description is not a substitute for confirming the actual consumable and closure requirement.

Define repeated handling

If two straps or crossed directions are needed, describe the movements between applications and who performs them. Include loading and removal from the work area. This separates a single strapping station from a request for automatic positioning, turning or coordinated transfer equipment.

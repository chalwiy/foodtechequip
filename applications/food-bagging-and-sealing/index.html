Food Bag Filling and Sealing Tasks

First decide whether the task starts with packaging film or a premade bag. Forming and filling a sachet is different from closing an already-filled bag, even when both operations handle the same product.

Define the starting pack

A sachet brief needs the finished pack dimensions, seal layout and film construction. A premade-bag brief starts with the bag supplied to the operator or machine. Make that distinction before comparing equipment names, because it changes the operations that must be included.

Match the feed to the material

The catalogue contains distinct granule and liquid sachet products. Provide the portion and material condition for the corresponding configuration. A powder, grain and paste should not be assumed to share the same feeding arrangement merely because the finished bags look similar.

Support the filled bag during closure

For a separate band sealer, measure filled height, weight and available mouth area. Show how the base rests while the mouth enters the sealing section. The weighing-and-bag-sealing product presents portioning and closing with conveyor transfer; describe operator tasks and the precise handover required.

Discuss the complete packaging result

Send material and packaging samples with your enquiry. Explain the closure result and output needed at that specific portion. Packaging compatibility and seal assessment need to address the actual construction; a broad plastic-bag description is not enough to specify a seal or establish shelf life.

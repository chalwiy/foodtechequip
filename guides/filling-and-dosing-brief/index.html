Define a Filling or Dosing Application

Start with the result you need: a measured portion, filled container or sealed sachet. Define material supply, receiving format and the next operation before comparing configurations.

Describe material at the working point

Provide a sample in its working condition. For liquids and sauces, describe consistency, temperature and particles. For powders and granules, explain flow and settling so feed and portioning requirements can be discussed.

State the portion and how it is checked

Specify the target mass or volume, checking method and output required at that portion. Compare these requirements with the data for the selected configuration.

Choose the receiving format

For bottles, provide the opening and full container drawing. For bags, identify whether they arrive preformed or are made from roll film as part of the requested process. For a dosing-only task, show how the receiving pack is supported and collected.

Show material supply and discharge

Indicate how material reaches the hopper or inlet and how empty containers arrive. Describe the next operation after filling or portioning. Use a simple process sketch to separate existing equipment, operator actions and items requested in the quotation.

Separate a format change from a material change

A new bottle size can change presentation and nozzle access; a new material can change the feed and preparation task. List those changes separately, even if they occur at the same time. Identify which containers and materials must share the equipment, the cleaning requirement between them and which setup items should be included. This gives the quotation a clear changeover scope without assuming automatic adjustment or stored recipes.

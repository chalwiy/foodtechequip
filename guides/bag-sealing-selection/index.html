Selecting a Bag or Tube Sealing Process

Define the material layers, intended seam and support needed for the filled bag or tube. These details help compare sealing processes and working arrangements.

Describe the complete construction

Provide polymer or fibre composition, laminates, coatings and layer structure. Include samples of nonwoven bags or film pouches with the intended seam marked.

Separate the joining process from handling

Ultrasonic joining uses vibration at the joint. Band sealing guides the bag mouth through a sealing section. Provide material samples and seam requirements to discuss the appropriate process and package presentation.

Include the filled package

State weight, height and available space above the contents. Ask how the pack reaches and leaves the sealing position. For tube equipment, include diameter, material and the condition of the open end. Where filling is also required, identify that operation separately.

Ask about the result you need

Show the intended seam in a drawing or sample. For vacuum or gas-flushing configurations, specify the required packaging atmosphere and finished-pack result alongside output at your pack format.

Describe handling before and after sealing

Explain whether the bag arrives filled, whether it remains upright and how it is supported during closing. For tube work, show both the incoming tube and required finished end.

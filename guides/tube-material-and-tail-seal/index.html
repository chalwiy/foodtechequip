Tube Filling and Tail Sealing: Material and End-Finish Choices

A squeeze-tube enquiry needs the tube construction and the open tail as well as the target fill. Closing the tail after filling is a separate task from applying a cap at the dispensing end.

Identify the tube construction

PE, other plastics, laminates and aluminium are different packaging constructions. Describe all layers and any coating instead of relying on the word tube. The FTE records include PE-tube filling and sealing equipment and plastic-tube ultrasonic sealing equipment. Start with those stated constructions and discuss the actual sample.

Separate a tail sealer from a filling-and-sealing machine

If tubes arrive already filled, the task may concern only positioning and sealing the open tail. A combined machine also has to deliver the product into the tube before closure. Identify whether filling is part of the requested scope, together with loading and collection; a tube sealer should not be assumed to include a dosing stage.

Define the end finish and presentation

Send an empty tube and a drawing or sample of the required closed tail. Include the diameter, total length and available tail area. Explain whether print alignment, trimming or code placement is required so those requests can be discussed explicitly rather than inferred from a catalogue image.

Keep the fill and package conditions together

Describe the material at filling, target quantity and how the filled tube reaches the closing position. Where a two-head filling configuration is considered, use its corresponding data. Compare the complete format and workflow rather than assuming all tube materials or product consistencies share one setup.

Details to prepare

Selection pointWhat to comparePractical reason
ConstructionLayers, material specification and tube sampleDefines the closing discussion
ScopeTail sealing only or filling plus sealingSeparates the equipment tasks
FinishClosed-tail sample, length and alignment requestDefines the requested appearance
FillMaterial condition and target quantityConnects dosing with package presentation

Include the tube, intended fill and desired tail finish in one enquiry. Discuss sample compatibility for the selected equipment before finalising the format.
